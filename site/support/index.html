Open Tasks

Support

Open Tasks is free and ad-free. Core use requires no account.

App help

Open More in the app for Privacy policy, import tools, and Backup & recovery. Core tasks, projects, schedules, reminders and focus timing work locally without Google authorisation.

Import and backup recovery

Use More → Backup & recovery for Google Drive backup or recovery, Android portable packages, and encrypted .otvault import or export. Keep your recovery phrase or passphrase private.

Public bug reports

Report reproducible problems at GitHub Issues. This is a public forum: do not post task contents, notes, attachment contents or names, exported files, recovery phrases or passphrases, credentials, Google account details, device identifiers, or other personal data.
